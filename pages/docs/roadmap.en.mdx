# Roadmap

> The token launches from strength — when the product and its security are ready, in the founder's judgment. Not a token sale, not a countdown, not a subscriber quota.

## Current status

**🚀 MVP live** at https://app.frostylabs.ai — visual workflow builder, AI chat, multi-chain wallet operations (1700+ chains via ThirdWeb), MCP integration, A2A / ERC-8004 agents, x402 micropayments, and the Frosty Engine for scheduled and triggered workflows.

**On-chain, on Base:** the revenue splitter, subscription contract (V2), and a 48-hour governance timelock are **deployed and source-verified**. They route a share of every payment to the POL reserve. See [POL](/docs/pol).

## Why there's no rush

$FROST is **earned by paying customers** — no presale, no VC, no insider round — so there is zero reason to rush the launch and every reason to ship it with live utility and real revenue. Each phase below produces the preconditions the next one needs.

## Phase 1 — Revenue foundation

Goal: real, on-chain revenue that funds the protocol and fills the POL reserve. *(Largely shipped.)*

- **USDC everywhere** — direct USDC subscriptions on Base (Pro $25 / Enterprise $99), replacing the old NFT model.
- **The five-bucket splitter** — every payment splits on-chain across POL, dev, affiliate, operating, and DAO in one transaction. *Live.*
- **Pay-per-deploy** — $5 / agent / 30 days to deploy and run non-LLM workflows.
- **Affiliate program** — 3-level referral attribution plus an earnings & links dashboard.
- **POL reserve** — a public address that accumulates USDC and is never spent.
- **Deploy any workflow as an agent** — x402-monetized, ERC-8004-registered, from the builder.

## Phase 2 — Growth & FrostyG4

Goal: grow paying subscribers and build the token's preconditions.

- **Expand use cases** to grow subscribers — data agents, live-trading and monitoring templates, more DEX and chain nodes.
- **Train & validate FrostyG4** — FrostyFi's own fine-tuned model (Gemma-based), starting small and graduating up. Until it beats the best stock model, we route to stock models — no premature infrastructure.
- **Build the token contracts** — the FROST token, the revenue-linked emission ledger, the PERMA lock, and a minimal USDC bond market.
- **Publish the full docs** — tokenomics, POL, funding, and this roadmap. *(In progress.)*

## Phase 3 — Token & POL launch

Goal: turn accumulated revenue into a fair-ish token launch and permanent liquidity.

- **$FROST launch** — accrued, revenue-linked emissions become claimable. Distribution is finite and earned by paying customers; no presale, no auction. See [Tokenomics](/docs/tokenomics).
- **POL reserve → permanent liquidity** — the entire USDC reserve is deployed as protocol-owned FROST/USDC liquidity, and every subsequent payment keeps market-buying into it. See [POL](/docs/pol).
- **Lock FROST → PERMA → USDC yield** — soulbound locks earn a share of POL trading fees in USDC, plus votes and product discounts.
- **FrostyG4 priced in FROST** — real utility on day one, 100% burned on use.

## When it launches

There is no subscriber quota and no calendar date. The token launches when the founder judges the product and its security are ready. The inputs weighed:

1. **Contracts built and tested end-to-end** — the emission, lock, and POL flows.
2. **A small on-chain reserve floor** to seed launch liquidity.
3. **Confidence the product stands on its own** — *"one subscriber is proof; a thousand is scale."*

<Callout type="info">
The old "≥1,000 subscribers before launch" gate is retired: there are no subscribers without a reason to buy, and no token without subscribers. FROST breaks that deadlock — it *is* the reason, earned by paying for a product that already works.
</Callout>

## The commitment

> **No presale, no pre-mine, nothing at launch — the dev earns a disclosed 10%, minted only as paying customers earn theirs, on the exact same claim terms as every customer.**

Every term — the emission schedule, the claim vesting, the dev reward, and the full unlock curve — is published **before** anyone earns a single token. Disclosed up front, it is a known term of the program; announced later, it would be a rug.
