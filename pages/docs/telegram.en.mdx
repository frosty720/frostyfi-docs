# Telegram

Chat with your [assistants](/docs/assistants) from Telegram: send messages, photos and
voice messages, switch between assistants, and get your scheduled jobs' answers on your
phone. Telegram runs on your FrostyFi account, so an assistant's memory, skills and tools
work the same as in the app.

## Connect

1. Open one of your assistants and find the **Telegram** panel. Click **Connect Telegram**.
2. Click **Open Telegram**, then tap **Start** in the chat with the FrostyFi bot. The link
   works once, for 15 minutes.
3. Back in FrostyFi, the panel shows the Telegram account asking to connect. Check that it's
   yours, then click **Confirm** (or **Reject**). Only confirm your own account: whoever you
   confirm can chat with the assistant as you.
4. The bot confirms in Telegram. Messages you send it now go to that assistant.

![The Telegram panel of an assistant, before connecting](/screenshots/assistant-telegram.png)

![Connecting in Telegram: tap Start, then confirm in FrostyFi](/screenshots/telegram-connect.png)

One Telegram account connects to one FrostyFi account, and the bot only answers in private
chats. Your Telegram conversations are saved as chats in FrostyFi too.

The bot writes to you in your Telegram app's language.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/new` | Start a fresh conversation |
| `/agents` | List your assistants |
| `/use 2` | Switch to number 2 on that list (or type part of its name) |
| `/unlink` | Disconnect this chat from FrostyFi |
| `/help` | Show how the bot works |

![The bot confirms the connection, and /agents lists your assistants](/screenshots/telegram-agents.png)

You can also switch from the app: open another assistant and click **Use this assistant in
Telegram** in its Telegram panel.

## Messages, photos and voice

- **Text**: the assistant answers in Telegram; long answers are split into several messages.
- **One at a time**: while it's answering, a new message gets "Still working on your last
  message…". Send it again once the answer arrives.
- **Photos**: send a photo, with a caption if you like, and the assistant sees it.
- **Voice messages** (Pro and Enterprise): the bot first replies with
  "🎤 I heard: …" so you can spot a mishearing, then the assistant answers your words.
  Voice messages are heard by Gemini 3.8 Flash and use your plan's credits; up to 5 minutes
  each. On the Free plan the bot asks you to send text instead.
- The assistant always answers in text. Stickers, files and videos aren't read yet.

![A voice message: the bot shows what it heard, then the assistant answers](/screenshots/telegram-voice.png)

## Scheduled answers

When an assistant has [scheduled jobs](/docs/assistants), each answer is also sent to your
Telegram chat. Reply to it to follow up: the conversation continues where the job left off.

## What runs from Telegram

For safety, some things only run in FrostyFi itself:

- **Workflows that can move funds** and **MCP tools** never run from Telegram. If someone
  got into your Telegram account, your wallets would stay out of reach.
- **Steps that need your approval** (testing, publishing, deploying, pausing, resuming and
  scheduling) are done in the app; the assistant will point you there.
- **Workflows that post** (X, Telegram, Discord, SMS, email) do run from Telegram.

Telegram chats with the bot aren't end-to-end encrypted: your messages and the assistant's
answers pass through Telegram's servers.

## Disconnect

Send `/unlink` in Telegram, or click **Disconnect** in an assistant's Telegram panel.
