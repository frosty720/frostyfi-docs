# Roadmap

> One token for everything FrostyLabs builds: $FROSTY launches on BaseStonk, with no presale and no minting. The products don't need it; it shares in what they earn.

## Current status

**🚀 MVP live** at https://app.frostylabs.ai — visual workflow builder, AI chat, multi-chain wallet operations (1700+ chains via ThirdWeb), MCP integration, A2A / ERC-8004 agents, x402 micropayments, and the Frosty Engine for scheduled and triggered workflows.

**On-chain, on Base:** the revenue splitter, subscription contract (V2), and a 48-hour governance timelock are **deployed and source-verified**. They route a share of every payment to the treasury.

## Phase 1 — Revenue foundation

Goal: real, on-chain revenue that funds the protocol. *(Largely shipped.)*

- **USDC everywhere** — direct USDC subscriptions on Base (Pro $5 / Enterprise $25), replacing the old NFT model.
- **The revenue splitter** — every payment splits on-chain across affiliates, operating, dev and the treasury in one transaction. *Live.*
- **Pay-per-deploy** — $5 / agent / 30 days to deploy and run non-LLM workflows.
- **Affiliate program** — 3-level referral attribution plus an earnings & links dashboard.
- **Deploy any workflow as an agent** — x402-monetized, ERC-8004-registered, from the builder.

## Phase 2 — Growth & FrostyG4

Goal: grow paying subscribers.

- **Expand use cases** to grow subscribers — data agents, live-trading and monitoring templates, more DEX and chain nodes.
- **Train & validate FrostyG4** — FrostyFi's own fine-tuned model (Gemma-based), starting small and graduating up. Until it beats the best stock model, we route to stock models — no premature infrastructure.

## Phase 3 — $FROSTY

Goal: one token tied to every product, with fixed, public terms.

- **Launch on BaseStonk** — 1B fixed supply, paired with USDC, launch liquidity nobody can pull. A 1% buy / 3% sell tax, split equally between holders (in USDC), auto-liquidity, the treasury and a disclosed team share. Fixed forever at launch. See [$FROSTY](/docs/tokenomics).
- **Treasury buybacks** — part of what the treasury earns buys FROSTY and burns it.
- **Research discount** — FROSTY holders pay less for FrostyFunds research.
- **Fund protocol fee in FROSTY** — fund managers pay the protocol fee in FROSTY at half price, and it is burned.

## The commitment

> **No presale, no VC, no minting.** The team's share of the tax is disclosed on the [$FROSTY](/docs/tokenomics) page, and every rate is fixed in the pool's contract.
