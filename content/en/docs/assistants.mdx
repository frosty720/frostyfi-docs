# Assistants

An **Assistant** is a chat agent you set up once and talk to whenever you need it: in
chat, from [Telegram](/docs/telegram), or on a schedule. It has a role and instructions,
uses skills, tools and your workflows, remembers you across conversations, and learns from
your chats.

> An assistant has no workflow behind it. A workflow you deploy as a callable, paid
> endpoint is an **Agent**, covered in [Agents, A2A & Payments](/docs/agents). The
> difference is explained in [Core Concepts](/docs/concepts).

![The assistant editor: name, description, role, instructions and tools, with a Try it chat on the right](/screenshots/assistant-editor.png)

## Create an assistant

Open **Assistants** in the sidebar and create one. Give it:

- a **name**, an **icon** and a short **description**;
- **Expert in**: its role, in a few words;
- **instructions**: its personality, guidelines and what it knows.

You can also describe what you want and let **Generate With AI** fill the form in.

The **Try it** panel on the right chats with the saved assistant, tools included, so you can
check how it answers before using it.

## Chat with an assistant

In the main chat, click **Agent** in the message box and pick one, or type `@` and choose it
under **Agents**. Clicking an assistant in the sidebar's **Assistants** list also starts a
chat with it.

![The Agent button in the chat box, listing the assistants you can chat with](/screenshots/chat-agent-picker.png)

The list shows your own assistants and public ones you can use. Paid workflow agents aren't
in it: they're called through their agent endpoint instead.

## Tools, skills and helpers

Under **Add tools that this assistant can use**, attach what it may use:

- **Skills**: instruction-only guides, such as a review checklist or a way of reasoning (see
  [Core Concepts](/docs/concepts)). The assistant loads a skill when your request needs it.
- **Workflows**: your published workflows, which the assistant can run as tools.
- **MCP tools** and **app tools** such as web search.
- **Other assistants** (helpers): add your other assistants and this one can hand them tasks.
  A helper answers with its own instructions and skills and reports back. Only your own
  assistants can be helpers, and a helper doesn't call helpers of its own.

![Tools attached to an assistant: a skill, a workflow and a helper assistant](/screenshots/assistant-tools.png)

## Working with your workflows

Your own assistant can work with your workflows for you. Ask it in plain words to:

- find a workflow and **run** it;
- **build** a new workflow, or **edit** one (saved as an unpublished draft for you to review);
- **test** a draft and **publish** it;
- **deploy**, **pause** and **resume** a deployed workflow;
- check **run history**: recent runs, failures and why they failed.

Steps with real effects ask first. Testing, publishing, deploying, pausing, resuming and
scheduling show **Approve** and **Reject** buttons in the chat, and nothing happens until
you click **Approve**. Drafts with steps that move funds, send messages, or call MCP tools or
other agents can't be tested or published by the assistant: it gives you a link to do that
in the editor. Deploys use your plan's included slots; the assistant never pays for a deploy.

### Approving tool calls

The chat's tool mode (the settings icon next to **Tools**) decides how tools are used:
**Auto** uses them freely, **Manual** asks before every MCP or workflow call, and **None**
uses no tools (an `@mention` still works). In Manual mode each call waits with its request
shown until you click **Approve** or **Reject**.

![A workflow call waiting for approval in Manual tool mode](/screenshots/chat-approve.png)

## Memory

Each assistant keeps a **Memory** about you, in two blocks:

- **Notes**: facts and context worth keeping, up to 2,200 characters.
- **About you**: who you are and how you like to work, up to 1,375 characters.

The assistant updates its memory itself as you chat. You can edit or clear it in the
**Memory** panel; one entry per line. Memory is kept per person: if someone else chats with
your public assistant, they get a memory of their own.

Lines the assistant wrote are listed under **Learned from your chats**. Click **×** to take
one out of the box, then **Save**. A line you type or edit yourself stays yours: the learning
loop never changes it.

![The Memory panel with lines the assistant learned from your chats](/screenshots/assistant-memory.png)

## Learning

Every 10 messages you send an assistant (in the app or on Telegram), it reviews the chat and
saves what it learned about you to memory. It only adds lines. When a memory block is nearly
full, it tidies up by merging or removing lines it learned itself, never yours.

When you teach it a repeatable way of doing something, such as a weekly recap in a fixed
order, it writes a **skill** for it. New skills wait under **Skills waiting for approval**:
click **Show steps** to read one, **Approve** to attach it to the assistant, or **Reject** to
discard it. Up to 3 can wait at a time. Read each one before approving: the assistant
follows its steps in your chats.

![The Learning panel with two skills the assistant wrote, waiting for approval](/screenshots/assistant-learning.png)

To stop it, turn off **Learn from my chats**; messages not reviewed yet are discarded.
Reviews run on your plan's default model and count toward your usage. Scheduled jobs and
other people's chats with your assistant are never learned from.

## Scheduled jobs

An assistant can answer a prompt on a schedule, for example "every weekday at 8:00, summarize
the crypto news". In the **Scheduled jobs** panel, write what it should do and pick when:
**Every day**, **Weekdays**, **Every week**, or **Every few hours**, at a time in your time
zone. Each answer is saved as a chat, and sent to your Telegram when it's connected.

![The Scheduled jobs panel with a weekly job](/screenshots/assistant-schedule.png)

From the job you can run it now, edit it, pause it or delete it. You can also ask the
assistant in chat to schedule something; it asks you to approve first.

| | **Free** | **Pro** | **Enterprise** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scheduled jobs running at a time (across all assistants) | — | 3 | 10 |

Scheduled runs can't move funds or use MCP tools, and each run counts toward your usage like
a message. Workflows that post (X, Telegram, Discord, SMS, email) do run.

## Telegram

Connect an assistant to Telegram from its **Telegram** panel and chat with it from your
phone, photos and voice messages included. See [Telegram](/docs/telegram).

## Deploy as a paid agent

The **Deploy as a paid agent** panel creates an agent workflow that runs this assistant
live. You then deploy that workflow from the workflow page, and callers pay per call with
x402.

![The Deploy as a paid agent panel, with a warning about attached workflows](/screenshots/assistant-deploy.png)

The panel warns you when attached workflows contain steps that send messages, call external
services, run paid tools or move funds. In calls from paying callers, steps that move your
funds or sign with your wallets are refused; everything else runs. Don't put secrets in an
assistant you deploy: a paying caller can ask the model to reveal its instructions and
skills. More in [Agents, A2A & Payments](/docs/agents).
