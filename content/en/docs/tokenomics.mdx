## The trading tax

Every trade pays a small tax, and where it goes is set in the pool's contract at launch. **The rates and the split can never be changed, by us or anyone else.**

| | Buy | Sell |
|---|---|---|
| Tax | 1% | 3% |
| BaseStonk's platform cut | 0.5% | 1% |
| FROSTY's share | 0.5% | 2% |

FROSTY's share is split four equal ways:

| Destination | Share | Of a buy | Of a sell | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Holders** | 25% | 0.125% | 0.5% | Paid to FROSTY holders in USDC |
| **Auto-liquidity** | 25% | 0.125% | 0.5% | Added to the pool; deepens it and can't be withdrawn |
| **Treasury** | 25% | 0.125% | 0.5% | The FrostyLabs Treasury Safe (`0x47b7…fF0A`), a 2-of-3 multisig |
| **Team** | 25% | 0.125% | 0.5% | The founder's wallet: a disclosed team share |

On a $100 buy followed by a $100 sell, the trader pays $4.00 in tax. BaseStonk keeps $1.50; holders, liquidity, the treasury and the team each get $0.625.

In the first minutes after launch, buys pay an anti-sniper toll that starts high and falls to the normal 1%. Sells never pay it, and the toll goes through the same split.

## What FROSTY is for

### Holder rewards, in USDC
A quarter of FROSTY's tax share is paid to holders in USDC, automatically, under BaseStonk's rules. You don't stake or lock anything.

### Research discount (coming)
FrostyFunds research (AI briefs and ratings on a fund's assets) will cost less for FROSTY holders. The discount and the balance that qualifies will be published here when research goes live.

### Fund protocol fee paid in FROSTY (planned)
FrostyFunds can charge funds a small yearly protocol fee (capped at 1% in the contract; it is 0% today). Once it is switched on, a fund's manager can pay it in FROSTY at **half price**, and that FROSTY is burned. The fee will be announced before it starts.

### Treasury buybacks
The treasury earns from the funds terminal (a 0.05% fee on fund spot swaps, a share of yield vault fees, perps referral rebates), from FrostyFi subscriptions and x402 payments, and from its share of the FROSTY tax. Part of that income buys FROSTY from the pool, and the FROSTY is burned.

## What FROSTY is not

- **No locking, no staking, no voting.** The earlier PERMA lock, bonds and on-chain governance design is retired.
- **No minting.** The supply is fixed at launch.
- **Not required.** Every FrostyFi and FrostyFunds product works without it. Subscriptions and fees are paid in USDC.
